## Methodology

The archive stores third-party market observations and does not set or guarantee the FINDEXY price. Daily values may differ slightly between providers because of data-collection time, pool routing, rounding, and indexer methodology.

The CSV is the machine-readable source for this section. New completed UTC days should be appended without rewriting earlier observations unless a source correction is documented.
