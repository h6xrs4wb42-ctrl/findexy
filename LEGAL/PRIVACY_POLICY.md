## 2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with FINDEXY, we may process:

### Information you provide

- your name or username;
- your email address;
- information contained in enquiries or support requests;
- information and evidence voluntarily submitted for attribution verification;
- wallet addresses and transaction identifiers submitted by you.

### Technical information

Our hosting and security providers may automatically process:

- IP address;
- browser and device type;
- operating system;
- date and time of access;
- referring page;
- pages visited;
- diagnostic and security logs.

### Public blockchain information

FINDEXY may analyse publicly available blockchain data, including wallet addresses, transaction hashes, token transfers, smart-contract interactions, relationships between blockchain addresses and infrastructure, and historical on-chain activity.

Blockchain records are public and generally immutable. Although a wallet address does not directly identify an individual, it may constitute personal data when connected to an identifiable person.

## 3. Purposes of Processing

Information may be processed to:

- operate and secure the website;
- respond to enquiries and support requests;
- prevent fraud, abuse and technical attacks;
- improve FINDEXY services;
- conduct blockchain transaction tracing and infrastructure research;
- assess wallet-attribution evidence;
- create and maintain attribution records;
- process correction and dispute requests;
- comply with applicable legal obligations;
- establish, exercise or defend legal claims.

FINDEXY attribution records and risk indicators are analytical outputs. They do not constitute legal, criminal, judicial or regulatory determinations.

## 4. Legal Bases

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, FINDEXY may rely on consent, performance of a contract or requested pre-contractual steps, compliance with legal obligations, legitimate interests in operating and securing the website, legitimate interests in analysing public blockchain activity and preventing fraud, and the establishment, exercise or defence of legal claims.

## 5. Cookies and Analytics

FINDEXY may use essential cookies necessary for website operation, security and functionality. Non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technologies will be used only where permitted by law and, where required, after obtaining consent.

Third-party websites and embedded services operate under their own privacy and cookie policies.

## 6. Sharing of Information

FINDEXY may share information with hosting and infrastructure providers, cybersecurity and technical service providers, professional advisers, auditors, compliance providers, public authorities where legally required, and parties involved in investigating misuse or resolving an attribution dispute.

FINDEXY does not sell personal data.

## 7. International Transfers

Service providers may process information outside your country of residence. Where required, appropriate legal safeguards will be used for international data transfers.

## 8. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for website operation and security, research and verification, dispute resolution, compliance with legal obligations and defence of legal rights.

FINDEXY cannot alter or delete public blockchain records. FINDEXY may, where appropriate, correct, restrict, mark as disputed or remove its own off-chain labels and analytical records.

## 9. Data Security

FINDEXY applies reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to prevent unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration or loss of information. No website, blockchain system or internet transmission can guarantee absolute security.

## 10. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to receive information about processing, access your personal data, correct inaccurate information, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, request data portability, withdraw consent and lodge a complaint with a competent authority.

Certain rights may be limited where information must be retained for security, legal compliance, legitimate research or dispute resolution.

## 11. Attribution Corrections and Disputes

If you believe a FINDEXY attribution, wallet label or risk indicator is inaccurate, contact <support@findexy.com>.

Please include the relevant blockchain, wallet or contract address, FINDEXY record ID if available, the disputed information, the reason for your request and supporting evidence.

Never send FINDEXY your private key, seed phrase, password or authentication code.

## 12. Children

FINDEXY is not directed to children and does not knowingly seek to collect personal data from individuals below the applicable legal age.
