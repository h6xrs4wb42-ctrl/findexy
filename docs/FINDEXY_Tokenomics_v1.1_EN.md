## 2. Contract-Level Monetary Rules

FINDEXY uses a fixed-supply OpenZeppelin ERC20 architecture. It has no owner or administrator, privileged roles, blacklist, pause, transfer tax/fee, max transaction/wallet limit, proxy or upgradeability. The FINDEXY contract does not expose a public token burn function and contains no external calls.

## 4. Allocation Is Not Circulation

Total supply, allocated supply, locked supply, treasury/reserve supply, liquidity allocation, initial liquidity deployment and circulating supply are separate concepts.

The 15% liquidity allocation does **not** mean that 3.15 billion FINDEXY will be deployed into a DEX at launch.

## 6. Team Lock and Vesting

Target policy: a **12-month lock from the official FINDEXY launch date, followed by 24 months of linear vesting**.

The base FINDEXY token contract does not enforce this policy. Any adopted vesting mechanism must be separately published and verifiable before the tokens are described as locked.

## 7. Liquidity Policy

The 15% Liquidity allocation is a ceiling for liquidity purposes, not a statement that 3.15 billion FINDEXY will be placed into a DEX at launch.

Initial Liquidity Deployment will be published separately with the official DEX, pair, pool address, deployed token amount and ownership/lock information for the liquidity position.

## 8. Token Utility

FINDEXY is intended as a utility token for FINDEXY products and services as they become operational. Potential utility includes advanced analytics, API/data access, attribution requests, verification services and enterprise or ecosystem functionality where technically and legally appropriate.

Token ownership does not itself create a right to fixed interest, guaranteed yield, guaranteed appreciation or predetermined investment return.

## 9. Shariah-Oriented Principles

FINDEXY requires its own independent Shariah assessment before any claim of Shariah compliance.

Relevant principles include fixed/public supply, no interest-bearing entitlement embedded in ownership, no guaranteed return, transparent allocation and utility tied to documented services.
